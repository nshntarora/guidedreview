## 2. Personal information collected

For purposes of this Privacy Policy, "Personal Information" means information that can be used to personally identify the User, including but not limited to a User's name, email address, or similar identifiers.

**Personal Information collected on the Website** — We may collect Personal Information from you when you (a) visit our Website, (b) contact us by email or other channels we publish, or (c) otherwise interact with materials on the Website. Information collected may include your name, email address, and the content of your message.

**Information processed by the Extension (on your device)** — The Extension is designed so that data needed to operate the product is processed primarily on your device. Depending on how you use the Extension, this may include:

- credentials and preferences you supply so the Extension can call services you choose (for example an LLM Provider API key and model settings);
- optional authentication credentials that allow the Extension to interact with GitHub on your behalf (for example to post review comments you submit);
- local review session information so you can continue a review of a pull request you have already started.

Under ordinary use of the Extension, this information is not sent to Artery Ventures as part of the core review workflow. We do not operate a product backend that receives your API keys or stores your pull request content for that workflow.

**Pull request content and third-party processing** — When you run a guided review, the Extension obtains the relevant pull request content from GitHub and may send that content (and related context needed to generate a review plan) to the LLM Provider you configured. That processing is subject to the provider's terms and privacy policy. We do not control how your chosen LLM Provider stores or uses that data. You are responsible for ensuring you are authorized to share repository and pull request content with that provider.

**GitHub** — Interactions with GitHub (obtaining pull request content, optional authentication, and submitting reviews) are subject to GitHub's terms and privacy policy. Those interactions occur between you, your browser, GitHub, and (where you have configured one) your LLM Provider.

**Social Media Platforms** — If you contact us through social media platforms such as LinkedIn, Twitter/X, or others, we may receive your publicly available profile information such as your name, email, and handle.

**Log Data and Website analytics** — When you visit the Website, our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, referring page, pages visited, and similar log or request data needed to serve and secure the site. We also use a third-party analytics service on the Website to understand general traffic and improve content. That analytics relates to Website visits and selected interactions with marketing materials; it is not used to track Extension usage while you review code on GitHub. The Extension does not send analytics or usage telemetry to us; it communicates with GitHub and your configured LLM Provider as described above. See our [Cookies Policy](https://guidedreview.dev/cookies) for more detail.

**Payment Information** — The Platform is currently offered without a paid subscription. If we later introduce paid features, payment card details would be handled by a third-party payment processor and not stored by us; we would update this Privacy Policy before collecting such information.
